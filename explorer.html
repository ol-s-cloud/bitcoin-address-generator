Public activity.

COBRA Explorer is the public intelligence layer for addresses voluntarily contributed to the COBRA network. Private keys, WIFs, seed phrases and entropy are never part of the registry.

Privacy boundary. Address publication is opt-in. Private/local generation remains outside the Explorer. The current launch shell starts at zero and is ready to be connected to the shared COBRA registry/backend.
