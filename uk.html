COBRA turns meter, tariff and asset data into an operating view centred on the economic use of electricity.

01Measure

Bring consumption, generation and tariff information into one site history.

02Compare

See when electricity is expensive, cheap, carbon-intensive or unusually high for the site.

03Model

04Act

Identify when flexible loads should run, shift, store, export, wait or curtail.
