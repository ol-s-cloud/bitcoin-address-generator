COBRA HOME

Your electricity, in context.

COBRA Home combines household energy data with tariff, grid and asset information so a meter reading becomes an operating picture rather than another chart.

UsageSee what your home uses

Track electricity and gas consumption by day, week and month, with half-hourly detail where the connected data source supports it.

CostSee what it costs

Apply your tariff to consumption, identify expensive periods and compare how timing changes the cost of the same electricity.

FlexibilityFind better operating windows

Identify loads that can move to lower-cost periods and quantify the value of shifting them.

AssetsModel before you buy

Test solar, batteries, EV charging, heat pumps and flexible compute against your own consumption profile.

DevicesConnect compatible equipment

CONNECTED ENERGY

From smart meter to IoT asset.

COBRA treats each meter or device as an asset attached to a site, with a defined identity, data source, operating state and permitted actions.

01SiteHome, office, shop, warehouse, mine, data centre or project.
02ConnectionSmart meter, supplier API, interval file, gateway or device API.
03AssetSolar, battery, EV, heat pump, appliance, miner, server or generation.
04COBRATariff, grid, market, telemetry and site economics.
05DecisionRUN · WAIT · SHIFT · STORE · EXPORT · CURTAIL.

Household smart-meter access requires the customer's permission and a compatible supplier or authorised data route. Device connectivity depends on the supported meter, gateway or manufacturer interface.
